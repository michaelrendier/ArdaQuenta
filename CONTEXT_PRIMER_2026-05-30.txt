engine/hamiltonian.py:

    The lossless semantic engine. No loops. No branches. Continuous flow.
    Classical orbit: xp = E (hyperbola = the semantic prime)
    Time evolution: x(t) = x₀·eᵗ,  p(t) = p₀·e⁻ᵗ
    Scale invariant: x→λx, p→p/λ, H→H (same at every language)
    Riemann zeros γₙ: the formant structure — eigenvalues of H=xp
    Three phases automatic:
      Classical orbit (xp=E)    → the semantic prime         [RED / Riemann]
      Quantum zeros (γₙ)        → the formant structure       [BLUE / Fermat]
      Time evolution (e^{iHt})  → the carrier waveform        [Yang-Mills]

  FermatEllipticHamiltonian — H_Blue = ½p² + ℘(x)
    The forbidden zone. Elliptic orbits (bounded, periodic — the repulsor).
    ℘(x) = Weierstrass elliptic function.
    Lagrangian L_Blue = ½ẋ² - ℘(x) breaks the mirror symmetry L_Red preserves.
    Wiles (1995) proved the Frey curve cannot exist → H_Blue has no real
    attractor at σ ≠ ½. The forbidden zone has no stable orbit.

  RedBlueHamiltonian — H_RB (the coupled system)
    J_Red  = +E   forward current — what IS

    Balance = J_Red + J_Blue   → 0 at σ = ½ (critical line)
    functional_equation_check() demonstrates ξ(s) = ξ(1-s) numerically.
